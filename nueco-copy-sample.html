Where it began

Jules grew up half-Colombian in England, raised in a family that treated wellness as a practice, not an industry. Her grandfather spent his career as a chemist at Cambridge, devoting decades to supplement research. That inheritance shaped everything The Nue Co. became: the belief that what you put in and on your body should be held to a higher standard.

"Clean" had no single definition. So Jules created one: formulas free from endocrine disruptors, synthetic fixatives, and unnecessary fillers. Products that work with your body's chemistry, not in spite of it.

The Silk Pen Copywriter's Notes What changed and why

The Hero

The live homepage opens with "Your Mood Rising" — a fragrance campaign line, not a brand statement. A first-time visitor has no idea who The Nue Co. is or why it is different from every other wellness brand. The rewrite leads with Jules's story: a Colombian family rooted in plant-based healing, a grandfather who devoted his career to supplement research at Cambridge. That origin is The Nue Co.'s strongest differentiator and it exists nowhere near the homepage. "Science that works with your body. Not against it." is a direct translation of the brand's founding philosophy into copy.

The Founder Strip

Jules's founder story lives on the About page. It is one of the most compelling founder stories in the wellness industry: half-Colombian, shaped by a grandfather chemist at Cambridge, started a brand because the industry had no standard worth trusting. That story converts a skeptical wellness consumer into a loyal one. This rewrite surfaces it as a dedicated homepage moment rather than letting it sit behind a navigation click most visitors never take.

The Fragrance Copy

The product names are extraordinary. Otherwhere. Forest Lungs. Water Therapy. Us. These are not supplement names. They are emotional states. Yet the product descriptions on the homepage barely expand on them. "Gourmand, Smoke, Comfort, Grounding" is a taxonomy label, not a feeling. This rewrite gives each fragrance a one-line copy moment that earns the name. "The breath you take when you finally stop moving" for Forest Lungs. "The feeling of the sea before you can see it" for Water Therapy. The names already did the heavy lifting. The copy just needs to land the feeling.

The Newsletter Capture

"Stay In The Loop. Sign up for all the latest info and offers in your inbox." is the current footer newsletter copy. For a brand with $35.9M raised, a philosophy of whole body health, and a founder who writes with genuine authority on wellness, "latest info and offers" is a significant mismatch in both ambition and voice. The rewrite positions the newsletter as a dispatch from Jules on what The Nue Co. is working on next. "Written when there is something worth saying. Never noise." is a direct reflection of how the brand thinks about its formulas: nothing unnecessary, everything intentional.
